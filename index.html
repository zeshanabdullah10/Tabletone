Mirror Piano

Play piano on any table. Your phone watches your fingers and listens for the taps.

  1. Stand the phone up facing you, screen toward you, on a bare table.
  2. Make sure both hands and the table in front of you are in view.
  3. Headphones help: the phone's speaker can trigger the mic.
