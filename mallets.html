Table Mallets

Your index fingers are the mallets. Point at a key on screen, tap the table, it plays.

  1. Stand the phone up facing you on the table, about an arm's length away.
  2. Rest your hands on the table so both index fingers show on screen.
  3. Move a finger over a key — it lights up — then tap the table with that finger.
